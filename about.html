About Bristol Community Events

Connecting Bristol residents and visitors with the best local events since 2024.

Our story

Bristol Community Events (BCE) was founded with a simple mission: to help people discover and book amazing local events across the city. We bring together exhibitions, workshops, sports, music and community gatherings in one accessible platform.

Today we connect thousands of residents and visitors with Bristol’s rich cultural scene — from intimate workshops at Arnolfini to large concerts at Ashton Gate.

Why choose BCE?

Our platform is designed with users in mind — easy booking, advance-booking discounts, student pricing, and real-time availability. Whether you are looking for a quiet workshop or a large music festival, BCE has something for everyone.

Legal & privacy

BCE is committed to protecting your personal data in line with UK GDPR. Passwords are stored as secure hashes and we never share your data with third parties. For questions, contact support@bceevents.co.uk.
